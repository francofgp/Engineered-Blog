{{/* JSON-LD Schema.org structured data — emitted in . This is the SINGLE most impactful AI-SEO / GEO change on the site: it's what Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, and Claude use to understand what each page IS (article? person? site?), WHO wrote it, and WHEN. Schema.org spec: https://schema.org/ — JSON-LD format docs: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/intro-structured-data Strategy: emit DIFFERENT schemas depending on page type. Per Google's guidance and John Mueller's "don't bloat" warning, we only mark up what the page actually IS — no FAQ/HowTo unless the post genuinely is one. Page-type matrix: .IsHome → WebSite (with SearchAction so AI knows about /search/) .Type == "about" → Person (the author entity; anchor for E-E-A-T) .Type == "post" → BlogPosting (+ nested author Person + publisher Organization + BreadcrumbList) everything else → (no schema; list pages / category pages don't benefit) Helpers used: jsonify (escapes properly), dateFormat (ISO 8601), absURL, dict, slice. IMPORTANT: never hand-concatenate JSON strings here — always build a dict and pipe through jsonify. Otherwise we'd ship broken JSON on any post with quotes/backslashes in its title.
